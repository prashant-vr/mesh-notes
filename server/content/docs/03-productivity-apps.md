# The 5 Productivity Apps

Mesh Notes includes 5 built-in productivity mini-apps. Rather than forcing you to download five separate apps with five separate databases, all 5 apps seamlessly read and write to your central notes stream.

---

## 1. Code Snippet Vault

Never lose a shell script, database query, or utility function again:
- **Automatic Indexing**: Any note containing fenced code blocks (```language ... ```) automatically appears in the Code Vault.
- **Language Filter**: Filter instantly by JavaScript, Python, SQL, Bash, Rust, Go, CSS, and more.
- **1-Click Copy**: Copy clean code to your clipboard with one tap.
- **Quick Snippet Creator**: Click "New Snippet" to write and tag a snippet without leaving the vault.

---

## 2. Daily Journal & Habit Tracker

Reflect on your day and build lasting habits:
- **One Page per Day**: Automatically opens today's note with a clean priorities and habits template.
- **Streak Tracker**: Calculates your active habit streak (`🔥 X Day Streak`) based on completed daily checklists.
- **Historical Calendar**: Use `< Prev` and `Next >` arrows or the date picker to review past journal entries.

---

## 3. Finances, Domains & Subscriptions

A clean personal cashflow tracker right in your notes:
- **Dual Cashflow**: Log expenses (`-$25 #groceries`) or income (`+$3000 #salary`). Mesh Notes calculates your total inflow, outflow, and net savings rate.
- **Multi-Country Currencies**: Select your local currency anytime:
  - 🇺🇸 **US Dollar ($ USD)**
  - 🇮🇳 **Indian Rupee (₹ INR)**
  - 🇪🇺 **Euro (€ EUR)**
  - 🇯🇵 **Japanese Yen (¥ JPY)**
  - 🇨🇳 **Chinese Yuan (¥ CNY)**
  - 🇷🇺 **Russian Ruble (₽ RUB)**
- **Domain & Account Tracking**:
  - Keep track of which email credentials and registrar are paying for which domain or subscription:
    ```markdown
    -$12.00 #domain provider:Cloudflare account:admin@mycompany.com domain:myapi.dev /yr Domain renewal #expense
    ```
  - Filter the entire subscriptions audit table by specific login email (e.g. view only `work@company.com` vs `personal@gmail.com`).
- **Category Budgets**: Set monthly spending limits for groceries, tech, or entertainment. Visual progress bars alert you in red if you exceed 100%.
- **Receipt Photos**: Attach photos of invoices or paper receipts directly to transactions.
- **CSV Export**: Export all transactions to standard `.csv` for Excel or Google Sheets.

---

## 4. Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)

Remember anything you learn using an Anki-style review deck:
- **Syntax**: Write `Q: Question \n A: Answer`, `Term :: Definition`, or tag with `#flashcard`.
- **Interactive 3D Flip Card**: Tap the card to reveal the answer.
- **Study Ratings**: Rate your recall with `Again`, `Good`, or `Easy` to adjust review frequency.

---

## 5. Highlights Inbox

Your personal quote library:
- While reading any article in **Reader Mode**, simply highlight a sentence with your cursor and click **Save Highlight**.
- Automatically saves with a quote block (`> "..."`), the `#highlight` tag, and the source web link.
- Review and copy your favorite passages in one central feed.
